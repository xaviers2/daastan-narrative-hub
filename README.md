# Daastan Narrative Hub

Build a modern, premium, single-page portfolio website for a communications agency named "Daastan Studio". The design should feel earthy, authentic, and grounded, using warm tones like terracotta, beige, sand, and deep brown. The typography should be elegant and readable.

Structure the page with the following sections:

Navbar: Logo text "DAASTAN STUDIO" on the left. Links to Approach, Services, Work, and a "Contact Us" button on the right.

Hero Section: A clean, spacious section with the headline "Weaving stories of real change." Subheadline: "Impact becomes communication people understand, remember, and act on." Include a primary button: "Book a Discovery Call".

The Problem Section: A two-column layout. Left side: "What's missing?" Right side text: "Most organizations have a communication team, but lack an outside lens to see the specific gap between the ground work and the audience."

How It Works (Grid): A 3x2 grid displaying the 6 steps: 01 Read, 02 Question, 03 Find, 04 Frame, 05 Build, 06 Travel. Use minimal, elegant icons for each.

Services: Three distinct cards titled: Story Strategy, Story Creation, and Story Amplification.

Featured Case Study: A highlighted section for "Kheyti - Farmer Education". Show the problem (teaching a technical process without field shoots) and the outcome (AI-generated instructional videos in Hindi for mobile adoption).

Who We Work With: A marquee scrolling text or simple list showing: NGOs, Foundations, CSR, Government Programmes, Social Enterprises.

Footer: Clean footer with the text "A story that carries meaning - that's what every daastan becomes." Include contact info: wearedastaan@gmail.com, +91 62683 97386, Bhopal.
